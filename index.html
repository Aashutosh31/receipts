What is Receipts?

Consistency has a history.

Receipts turns a promise to yourself into a 90-day record. It is not a body-punishment app or a motivational feed. It is a clear place to see what you committed to, what happened, and what pattern you are building.

How it works

Four honest steps.

  1. 01

    Commit

    Choose three to five daily commitments and sign a 90-day contract.

  2. 02

    Show up

    Use Today to log what you did. A check-in is final.

  3. 03

    Record it

    If you miss, name the reason. Tired, busy, unmotivated, forgot, or other.

  4. 04

    Review the receipts

    Your Ledger and Insights make the pattern impossible to blur.

The Ledger

Your past can’t be edited.

Every day has a place: kept, missed, paused, or upcoming. The Ledger is read-only and permanent, so the story stays honest after the moment passes.

What’s inside

Built for the full record.

01

Contract

Three to five commitments, 90 days, Hard or Kind Mode, and a typed-name pledge.

02

Today

Your daily commitments, streak, pause handling, and the only question that matters: did you do it?

03

Notifications

Target-time reminders, follow-ups, quiet hours, and a tone that can stay calm, firm, or blunt.

04

Insights

Excuse patterns, weekday breakdowns, weekly trends, and one clear line about what the record says.

05

Letters

Write on day 1. Reopen on days 30, 60, and 90—when server time says it is time.

06

Squads

A small, privacy-safe group for shared misses and streaks. No notes or commitment titles exposed.

07

Final Receipt

At day 90, see the kept rate, longest streak, top excuse, and the letter you wrote at the start.

The point

No shame. No rewriting.
Just an honest record.

Receipts is serious about consistency, not cruelty. The record is private. The truth is server-timed for the things that lock, unlock, and get dated. And a miss can be a miss without becoming a verdict on you.

Privacy & security

Your record is personal.

The app stores your account, contract, commitments, ledger, excuse tags and notes, pauses, letters, contract changes, squads, and nudges. Reminder preferences and the offline cache stay on your device. Receipts does not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising IDs, or analytics.

Row Level Security limits personal data to you. Squadmates see only the limited status information needed for a squad—not your letters, notes, excuse notes, or commitment titles.
